# Lumen — notes for Claude

- The whole game is `index.html` (canvas + vanilla JS, no build step). GitHub Pages deploys it from `main`.
- **Bump `VERSION` near the top of the script on every change** (semver-ish: minor for features, patch for fixes). It's shown on the title and pause screens so the user can confirm the live site updated.
- Feel/balance numbers live in `TDEF` (live-editable in game with T). Bump the `TKEY` suffix when defaults change meaningfully.
- Design intent: inspired by Hollow Knight but not a copy. No swipe attack, no pogo (locked ability flag only), no channelled heal. Light is the core verb.
- **Always merge automatically** (user's standing instruction): after pushing, open a PR to `main` and merge it yourself.
- The map is data: `buildOfficialGrid()` + the `*_DEF` lists form `officialMap()`; `loadMap()` loads any map. The in-game editor (M) saves a custom map to localStorage (`lumen.map.v1`) which overrides the official one in that browser. To adopt a user's exported map as official, replace the official data with it.
- The world is 400×200 tiles. The inner world (268×132) is built first: the original coast is carved by `buildOfficialGrid()` in its own 200×60 coordinates and placed at (`OX`, `OY`) = (64, 40), and `addRegions()` carves Bleached Dunes/Buried Ruins, Rime Hollows and Kelp Deep in inner coordinates. That inner world is then shifted by (`SX`, `SY`) = (66, 34) (`shiftMap`), and `addOuterRegions()` carves the outer ring in world coordinates: Glowcap Grove + Rootway (NW), Windworn Spires (N), Cinder Vents (E), The Abyss (S), Drowned Vault (SW), linked to each other and inward like a web. `m.areas` names regions and sets their theme (palette, background) and danger `tier` (1–5; the `TIER` table scales enemy HP, aggression and harder-variant odds; tier 1 never has harder variants).
- Enemies in map data can have `size` ('small'/'big', see `SIZES`) and `swarm: n` (n members, small by default).
- After map changes, check reachability with the editor's `edReach()` (via `window.__lumen`): every pickup, beacon, lamp and lore stone should be reachable and returnable. Two pre-existing flasks (Deep Oil Flask, Glass Heart) are flagged because the checker doesn't model their mechanics.
- Upgrade kinds (`UPG_KINDS`): fuel, mask, power (lens shard, beam damage %), range, wide, and abilities dash (shown as Flashstep: blink along the aim, Shift/L/touch Step button) and updraft (air jump, costs oil). Ability-gated secrets: Frost Loft and Flooded Annex (either ability opens each); the path checker flags them as unreachable by design — verify gates by simulation instead.
- Lit beacons are sanctuaries (`SANCT_R`): enemies and pools within reach are cleared and never return. With 2+ lit beacons, E at one opens the travel chart (`travel`/`travelFx`). The pause screen shows the Keeper's Chart, drawn only from tiles the light has touched (`seen`, `markSeen`).
- Avoid Hollow Knight terms in player-facing text: health units are wicks, never masks (internal names like `P.masks` are fine).
